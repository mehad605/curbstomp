Curbstomp is a lightweight, 100% on-device Android app blocker with zero accounts, zero telemetry, and no internet permission.

Create recurring schedules to block or allowlist apps during set hours (including overnight windows) with optional per-app daily time limits, or launch an Instant Focus session on demand.

Features:
- Scheduled blocklist & allowlist rules with overnight support and per-app daily quotas
- Instant Focus sessions (30m, 1h, or custom) with live countdown, pause/resume, time extension, and strict app editing
- Password protection (salted PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256) with persisted exponential brute-force lockout
- Anti-uninstall (Device Admin), locale-independent Accessibility self-protection, and Extra Harden Settings lock
- Event-driven Accessibility engine with zero idle background polling and a ~1.3 MB single-DEX APK

Everything stays strictly on your device.
